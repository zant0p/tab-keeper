🔒 Tab Keeper Privacy Policy

Version 2.0.3 Last Updated: June 26, 2026

Overview

Tab Keeper is a Chrome extension designed for enterprise and healthcare facility deployments. This privacy policy applies to all versions distributed through the Chrome Web Store (including private domain-only releases) and GitHub.

Quick Summary

What We Collect Nothing. No user data, no analytics, no tracking.
Credentials Stored locally via enterprise policy or user config. Never transmitted.
Third Parties None. No analytics, no ads, no data sharing.
Your Rights Uninstall anytime. All data deleted automatically.
